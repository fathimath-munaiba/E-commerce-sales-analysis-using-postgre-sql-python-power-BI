E-commerce Sales Analysis | SQL, Python & Power BI

Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing e-commerce transactional data to evaluate business performance, identify sales trends, understand customer purchasing behaviour and generate actionable business insights.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow using PostgreSQL, Python and Power BI, transforming raw data into meaningful information to support data-driven business decisions.

Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of transactional data through customer orders, product purchases, payments and returns. However, raw data alone does not provide a clear understanding of business performance.

The business needs to understand:

Which products generate the highest revenue?

How is revenue and profitability changing over time?

Which customers contribute significantly to sales?

How do product returns affect business performance?

Which payment methods are most commonly used?

The objective of this project is to analyse the available data, calculate key business performance indicators (KPIs) and identify insights that can support better business decisions.

Project Objectives

Analyse overall sales and profitability.

Calculate revenue, gross profit and gross profit margin.

Evaluate month-over-month revenue growth.

Identify top-performing products.

Understand customer purchasing behaviour.

Analyse orders, returns and payment methods.

Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard.
